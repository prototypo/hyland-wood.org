      How to use this map

      • The year. The map shows the world in one year. The year is in the box at the bottom. Move the slider to change the year. Press ‹ or › to go one year back or forward. You can also type a year, for example "500 BCE".
      • Play. Press ▶ and the years run. The names above the timeline are events of that time. Click a name to see the event.
      • The map. Drag the map to move it. Press + or − to zoom. Click a country to read about it.
      • Find. "Search" finds a place, an empire or a topic by its name. "Lists" has topics from school. "Shows" has maps of one subject, for example religion.
      • One thing at a time. When you pick a thing in "Find", the map goes to the first year that has it. Only that thing has its colours. The rest of the map is grey.
      • Clear. The button "Clear" at the timeline puts the map back to the start.
      • Details. Choose how much the map shows: Simple, More or Full.

      How sure is the map?

      Old borders are not exact. A dashed line is an estimate. A full line is a surveyed border. An area with stripes is disputed: more than one side says that it owns the area.
